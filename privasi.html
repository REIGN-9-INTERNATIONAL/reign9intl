Kebijakan Privasi & Ketentuan

Berlaku sejak 13 November 2026

Data yang kami kumpulkan

  • Dari akun Google saat kamu login: nama, alamat email, dan foto profil Google.
  • Yang kamu isi sendiri: IGN, UID, jenis pendaftaran, ID Discord dan/atau nomor WhatsApp, foto profil, screenshot karakter, bio, komentar, suka, dan link video TikTok.

Untuk apa data dipakai

  • Memproses pendaftaran member, roster, dan peserta turnamen oleh pengurus Reign 9.
  • Memberi link grup WhatsApp setelah pendaftaranmu diterima.
  • Menampilkan profilmu (IGN, foto, screenshot, bio) ke sesama member yang sudah diterima.
  • Menampilkan video TikTok yang sudah disetujui tim Media, beserta IGN dan foto profilmu, di Content Creator Hub reign9.site.

Siapa yang bisa melihat

Kontak (email, Discord, WhatsApp) dan UID hanya bisa dilihat pengurus Reign 9 sesuai tugas divisinya. Kami tidak menjual atau memberikan datamu ke pihak lain.

Penyimpanan & penghapusan

Data disimpan di server Reign 9. Kamu bisa minta data atau akunmu dihapus kapan saja lewat email reign9intl@reign9.site.

Ketentuan layanan

  • Isi data dengan benar. Akun palsu atau data orang lain bisa ditolak atau dinonaktifkan.
  • Jaga sopan santun. Komentar dan profil yang menghina, SARA, atau melanggar hukum bisa disembunyikan pengurus.
  • Video yang kamu kirim harus milikmu sendiri.
  • Pengurus berhak menerima atau menolak pendaftaran sesuai kebutuhan komunitas.

Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective 13 November 2026

Data we collect

  • From your Google account when you sign in: name, email address, and Google profile photo.
  • What you enter yourself: IGN, UID, registration type, Discord ID and/or WhatsApp number, profile photo, character screenshot, bio, comments, likes, and TikTok video links.

How we use it

  • To process member, roster, and tournament participant registrations by the Reign 9 admins.
  • To give you the WhatsApp group link once your registration is approved.
  • To show your profile (IGN, photo, screenshot, bio) to other approved members.
  • To feature TikTok videos approved by our Media team, with your IGN and profile photo, in the Content Creator Hub on reign9.site.

Who can see it

Contact details (email, Discord, WhatsApp) and UID are only visible to Reign 9 admins according to their division’s duties. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Storage & deletion

Data is stored on Reign 9’s server. You can ask for your data or account to be deleted at any time by emailing reign9intl@reign9.site.

Terms of service

  • Provide accurate information. Fake accounts or someone else’s data may be rejected or deactivated.
  • Be respectful. Abusive, discriminatory, or unlawful comments and profiles may be hidden by the admins.
  • Videos you submit must be your own.
  • The admins may approve or reject registrations based on the community’s needs.
